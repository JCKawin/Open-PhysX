# H1 — Domain, flow, and effector

Points: 3. Epic H. Depends on [E5](../epice/e5.md) and [G3](../epicg/g3.md) for collider meshes.

Blender spec: `DNA_fluid_types.h`. Domain types gas and liquid. This story is gas only. Flow behaviors inflow, outflow, geometry. Effector types collision and guide. Guide waits. Collision is the effector this story stores.

## What to do

Turn the fluid modifier into real settings for one gas domain, any number of flows, and collision effectors. No grid is stepped. The domain draws its bounds. Flows and effectors show their role in the panel.

## How to do

1. Domain payload: `int resolution` default `32`, maximum `64` for the first solver, `float time_scale` default `1`, `bool cache_outdated`.
2. Flow payload: `enum class FlowBehavior { Inflow, Outflow, Geometry }`, `float density` default `1`, `bool use_velocity`.
3. Effector payload: `float surface_distance` default `0`. The mesh is the collision modifier or the evaluated mesh.
4. One domain per scene, already enforced in [E5](../epice/e5.md). The domain object's box or mesh bounds are the grid bounds. Draw the bounds as a wire cube when the object is the domain, in addition to the object itself.
5. Physics tab shows the payload for the active object's fluid modifier. Baking controls stay disabled until [H3](h3.md).

## What to consider

- Resolution is per axis on the longest side. The other axes follow the bounds aspect so the cells are cubic. A very flat domain still has at least 4 cells on the short axis. Clamp and show the resulting cell counts in the panel.
- Do not allocate the 32³ grids in this story. [H2](h2.md) allocates them when a bake or play needs them.
- Liquid, fire, and guide are not in the enum yet. Leaving them out is the point.

## Output

The scene can contain one domain, a flow cube, and an effector cube. The panel edits resolution and behavior. A second domain is refused. The domain wire bounds match the object. Nothing moves in the grid, because there is no grid.

# H5 — Effectors follow rigid bodies

Points: 2. Epic H. Depends on [H2](h2.md), [H4](h4.md), and [G4](../epicg/g4.md).

Blender spec: fluid collision effectors sampled from the evaluated mesh each frame. One-way: the body blocks or displaces the gas. The gas does not push the body.

## What to do

Each gas step, rasterize collision effectors into the grid using their evaluated transforms after the rigid step. Cells covered by an effector get zero velocity and zero density, or they get a velocity taken from the rigid body if "use velocity" is on. The gas does not apply a force back.

## How to do

1. After the rigid step and before advection, for each effector:
   - Take the evaluated triangles or the primitive box in world space.
   - Mark covered cells. A box test is enough when the effector has no mesh. A mesh uses a coarse voxelization: any triangle that overlaps the cell marks it. Exact signed distance is not required.
   - Set velocity in those cells to the body's linear velocity if the flag is on, else to 0. Set density to 0.
2. Moving the rigid body and playing shows the empty region follow it. The point cache and the VDB cache both update, and both dirty when the effector transform changes under the user's hand while paused.
3. Do not read `velocity_at` to apply forces on the rigid solver. Leave a named function unused, or a comment at the call site that two-way coupling is the later item in `engine-path.md`. Calling it and ignoring the result is noise. Do not call it.

## What to consider

- Order is rigid step, then effector raster, then advect, then pressure. Raster after advection lets the gas enter the solid for a frame. Raster before.
- A fast body still tunnels if it crosses more than one cell per gas substep. The gas CFL cap from [H2](h2.md) covers gas speed. Also substep the raster when the body's motion per frame exceeds one cell. Cap at the same 4.
- One-way coupling will look like the smoke avoids the cube. It will not look like the cube is pushed. That is the acceptance test, not a bug.

## Output

A passive or animated cube marked as a collision effector leaves a hole in the puff, and the hole follows the cube. The cube's rigid motion does not change when the density changes. Scrubbing shows the hole from the VDB file. Two-way force is not implemented.

# H2 — Gas step

Points: 8. Epic H. Depends on [H1](h1.md) and [G1](../epicg/g1.md).

Blender spec: `BKE_fluid_modifier_do` and the gas solver in Mantaflow. Match the role of that function, not its source. One domain, gas only, CPU, capped grid.

## What to do

Step a gas grid: inflow writes density, a semi-Lagrangian step advects velocity and density, a pressure projection makes the velocity roughly divergence-free, outflow clears density. The grid lives on the domain's runtime data. Effectors do not move the gas yet. That is [H5](h5.md).

## How to do

1. Allocate `resolution`³ floats for density and three float grids for velocity, once, from a dedicated buffer on the domain, not from the frame arena. The arena is too small and is rewound. The grid is persistent. Cap the longest side at 32 for this story even if the panel allows 64. If the panel says 64, clamp and show "clamped to 32".
2. Each simulation frame, inside `step`, after the rigid step:
   - Clear nothing. The grid persists.
   - For each flow set to Inflow, rasterize its bounds into cells and add `density * dt`.
   - Outflow sets density and velocity to 0 in its cells.
   - Advect density and velocity with semi-Lagrangian backtrace and trilinear sample. CFL: if `speed * dt` is larger than one cell, substep the gas so it is not. Cap gas substeps at 4.
   - Pressure: Jacobi or Gauss-Seidel on a divergence Poisson, 20 to 40 iterations. Subtract the gradient from velocity.
   - Dissipate density by a small factor so a puff fades. A field `float dissolution` default `0.1` per second.
3. Domain bounds map world position to cell index. Outside the domain, samples are 0.
4. This runs only when the world is playing forward one frame, same rule as [G4](../epicg/g4.md). Mute on the fluid modifier skips it.
5. Do not write OpenVDB here. The grids are RAM. [H3](h3.md) writes them.

## What to consider

- A 32³ float grid of density plus three velocity channels is about 0.5 MB. Do not allocate per frame.
- Indexing is `x + resolution_x * (y + resolution_y * z)`. Test a cell at `(1,0,0)` so Y-up does not swap the loops by accident.
- The rigid solver and the gas solver share the frame clock. Gas reads transforms after the rigid write-back so a moving inflow uses the new position. Effector coupling is still [H5](h5.md). A static inflow is the test.
- No `new` inside the Jacobi loop. The pressure scratch buffer is allocated with the grids.

## Output

Play with a domain and an inflow fills cells. The density is non-zero in a test that samples the inflow cell after one second. An outflow on top of that cell returns it toward 0. Pausing leaves the grids in memory. The viewport may still show only the domain wire. Drawing is [H4](h4.md).

# G5 — Contacts, islands, iterations, sleep

Points: 8. Epic G. Depends on [G4](g4.md).

Blender spec: the Bullet step behind `RB_dworld_step_simulation`. The algorithm to implement is the scalar pipeline in `hardware.md`: broadphase, narrowphase, islands, solve, sleep. Box and sphere only for the contact math. Convex hull and triangle mesh can share the broadphase and return no contact until a later pass.

## What to do

Active boxes and spheres collide with each other and with passive boxes and spheres. They rest instead of sinking. Islands solve separately. A body that stops moving falls asleep and is skipped. The solver is scalar, on the sim thread, and uses the arena for pairs and contacts.

## How to do

1. Broadphase: sort and sweep on X, or a uniform grid. Output overlapping pairs of shape bounds. Allocate the pair list from the arena.
2. Narrowphase: box-box, sphere-sphere, box-sphere. Produce a contact with point, normal (from the second body toward the first, pick one and test it), and penetration depth. One contact per pair is enough for this story.
3. Islands: union-find on the contact graph. Sleeping bodies are not seeds. A contact with an awake body wakes the neighbor.
4. Solve: sequential impulses, `iterations` times per island. Apply impulse along the normal to remove penetration and inbound relative velocity, scaled by restitution. Friction is one tangent impulse clamped by `friction * normal_impulse`. Respect `linear_factor` and `angular_factor`. Update angular velocity from the lever arm.
5. Sleep: if linear and angular speed stay under the thresholds for a number of frames (use 8), mark the body asleep and stop integrating it until a contact or a user edit wakes it.
6. A static floor is a passive box the user can add, not a special case in the solver. Document a two-body test: cube dropped on a passive floor rests within a centimeter after one second.
7. All scratch is arena memory rewound at the end of `step`.

## What to consider

- Normals that flip sign launch the cube downward. The test is: a sphere resting on a box ends with the normal pointing up (+Y).
- Do not solve contacts in world order across islands. Islands are independent. Inside one island the order must be stable (sort by the two body ids) so a replay is deterministic.
- Triangle mesh and convex hull are not required to produce contacts here. If they are in the broadphase, skip the pair and do not crash.
- Iterations default `10` from [G2](g2.md). More iterations should reduce leftover penetration in the drop test, not change the sign of the normal.

## Output

An active cube dropped on a passive floor rests. Two spheres stack. A lone cube in the air falls asleep after it settles. The drop test is a function in the test binary or a documented scene file. No `::operator new` inside the substep loop.

# G7 — Joints

Points: 5. Epic G. Depends on [G5](g5.md) and [G6](g6.md).

Blender spec: `eRigidBodyCon_Type` in `DNA_rigidbody_types.h`. Used types: Point, Hinge, Slider, Piston, 6DoF, 6DoF spring, Fixed, Motor. Unused in Blender: cone twist, hinge2, universal, the simple spring. Skip those.

## What to do

A joint connects two rigid bodies and is solved in the island with the contacts. The types are Fixed, Point, Hinge, Slider, Piston, 6DoF, 6DoF spring, and Motor. Each joint can be enabled, can disable collisions between its two bodies, and can break. 6DoF has per-axis limits. The cache stores a broken flag per joint per frame.

## How to do

1. `Joint` on the scene, not on the modifier stack: `body_a`, `body_b`, `JointType`, frames as a local position and quaternion on each body, `bool enabled`, `bool disable_collisions`, `float break_impulse` (`0` means unbreakable).
2. 6DoF payload: six limit bools and min/max, matching Blender's linear XYZ and angular XYZ. Spring payload adds stiffness and damping per axis. Motor adds a target linear and angular speed and a max impulse.
3. Physics tab lists joints for the scene, or for the active body. Add chooses the other body. Drawing: when the joint overlay is on, draw the two frames as small axes.
4. In the solver, a joint is a row in the island. Fixed removes all relative motion. Point locks position and leaves rotation. Hinge locks position and allows one rotation axis. Slider allows one translation axis. Piston allows translation and rotation on the same axis. 6DoF clamps the six relative channels. Motor adds an impulse toward the target speed.
5. If the impulse magnitude exceeds `break_impulse` and the threshold is positive, set `broken` for this frame and skip the row afterward. Write `broken` into the point cache record.
6. Changing a joint dirties the cache the same way a mass change does.

## What to consider

- Joints are solved in the rigid world. They are not [F2](../epicf/f2.md) constraints. A Child Of and a Fixed joint on the same pair will fight. The panel can warn. Do not silently delete either.
- `disable_collisions` filters the pair out of the narrowphase. It does not remove the bodies from other contacts.
- Break state is per cached frame, not a permanent delete of the joint asset. The editor joint stays. The cached frame says it was broken.

## Output

A hinge between a passive base and an active plank swings in one axis and stops the other axes. Fixed keeps two cubes glued. A motor spins a hinge. Raising the break threshold low enough splits the joint during the fall, and scrubbing back shows it intact on earlier frames. Cone twist is not in the Add menu.

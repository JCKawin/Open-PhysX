Open PhysX — where we are

The editor is up. You can orbit the view, move the cube, undo, and play the timeline. That part is in place.

The physics is the work ahead. We are building it the way Blender does: objects in a scene, modifiers, constraints, rigid bodies, then fluid. Same ideas, our own code.

What you will see, in order

1. More than one object. A list, names, select, duplicate, delete.
2. Import a model (OBJ, STL, PLY) and save the scene.
3. On-screen move, rotate, and scale handles, and cleaner property panels.
4. Modifiers and simple constraints (copy location, limits, parent).
5. Rigid bodies that fall, collide, rest, and can be scrubbed back without resimulating.
6. Joints: hinge, slider, fixed, and a few others.
7. Gas in a box, saved as OpenVDB so playback is instant.

Cloth, liquid, and heavier formats (FBX, USD) come after the rigid-body cache and the fluid cache both play back cleanly.

What we are not building in this pass

A full Blender clone. No sculpt, no film renderer, no Python add-ons. The goal is a physics workstation that feels familiar if you know Blender.

The detailed build list is in docs/engine-path.md. Each step has its own short page under docs/epica through docs/epich.
